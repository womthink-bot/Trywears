## 1. Executive Summary & Methodology

As a factory-direct apparel manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan, TRY WEARS supplies private-label sports, activewear, streetwear, and leather outerwear to international markets. 

To eliminate speculative pricing and arbitrary markups, this pricing model is built upon **empirical market research** conducted across:
1. **Alibaba Verified Sialkot Suppliers & Exporters** (e.g., Baasta Industries, Fly Humble Sportswear, ZipaSports, Sialkot Fitnesswear, Habit Fit Sports, Combatwear Factory, Saamir Sports).
2. **Global Sources & Made-In-China Apparel Indexes**.
3. **Sialkot Chamber of Commerce & Industry (SCCI) Export Benchmark Cost Buildups**.
4. **Current Raw Material & Cut-Make (CM) Rates** in the Sialkot apparel manufacturing cluster.

### Cost Buildup Formula per Product:
$$\text{Wholesale FOB Unit Price} = \text{Fabric Cost} + \text{Trims \& Hardware} + \text{Cut \& Stitch (CM)} + \text{Decoration / Branding} + \text{QC \& Export Packaging} + \text{Factory Overhead \& Margin}$$

- **Tier 1 (25–49 units):** Accounts for pattern grading, individual tech-pack setup, machine changeover, and low-yield cutting wastage. Factory gross margin: ~28–32%.
- **Tier 2 (50–99 units):** Setup costs amortized; fabric bought in small rolls. ~12–15% volume discount from Tier 1.
- **Tier 3 (100–249 units):** Standard wholesale production run. Full fabric bolt utilization. ~22–25% volume discount from Tier 1.
- **Tier 4 (250–499 units):** Commercial batch run. Automated cutting efficiency and lower dye lot costs. ~30–35% volume discount from Tier 1.
- **Tier 5 (500+ units):** Large container / export run. Bulk yarn and mill rates applied. ~38–44% volume discount from Tier 1.

## 4. Five Wholesale Quantity Tiers Framework

All 2,372 physical samples in the TRY WEARS catalog are programmed with 5 active quantity tiers:

```
[Tier 1: 25–49 PCS/SETS]   --> Setup & Sampling Tier (Unit Price = P1)
[Tier 2: 50–99 PCS/SETS]   --> Brand Launch Tier (~12-15% discount)
[Tier 3: 100–249 PCS/SETS] --> Core Wholesale Tier (~22-25% discount)
[Tier 4: 250–499 PCS/SETS] --> Commercial Growth Tier (~30-35% discount)
[Tier 5: 500+ PCS/SETS]    --> High-Volume Master Tier (~38-44% discount)
```

Each product listing explicitly specifies whether the price is **PER PIECE** or **PER COMPLETE SET**, including exact garment composition.
